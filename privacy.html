Kindle Nest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Kindle Nest is a private app used by one household. It connects to the owner's own Google Nest thermostat through the Google Smart Device Management API.

What it uses

The app reads the thermostat's temperature, humidity, mode and set point, and changes the set point when the owner asks. It uses this only to show and control the thermostat on the owner's home dashboard.

What it stores and shares

It stores only the access token needed to reach the thermostat, on the owner's own computer. It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone, and it does not use data for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
